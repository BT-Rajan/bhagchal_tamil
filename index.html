Aadu Puli Aatam · The Goat & Tiger Game of Tamil Nadu

Aadus 15 to place
Pulis: captured 0/5

Rules · விதிகள்
  • Setup: 3 pulis (tigers) start on the apex and the two points just below it. 15 aadus (goats) start off the board.
  • Aadus move first by placing one goat on any empty point. Goats cannot move until all 15 are placed; then they slide one step along a line.
  • Pulis step to an adjacent empty point, or capture by jumping over one adjacent goat in a straight line onto an empty point, one goat per jump. They cannot jump other pulis.
  • Winning: pulis win by capturing 5 aadus. Aadus win by blocking all three pulis so none can move.
