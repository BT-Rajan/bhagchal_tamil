# Aadu Puli Aatam (ஆடு புலி ஆட்டம்)

Single-page Goat & Tiger game of Tamil Nadu, playable against the computer. Open `index.html` in a browser.

- 3 pulis (tigers) vs 15 aadus (goats) on the triangle board
- Aadus place first; they move only after all 15 are placed
- Pulis win by capturing 5 aadus; aadus win by blocking every puli
- Play either side; Easy / Medium / Hard (minimax AI); undo; sound toggle
